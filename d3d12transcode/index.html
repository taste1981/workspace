Chrome 158 · Windows · Direct3D 12 video encoding

HDR transcoding, with a reference structure you design

Five frames of HDR10 video are decoded on the GPU, re-encoded by Chrome's Direct3D 12 encoder with the reference structure you choose, and read back from the bitstream to show exactly what the encoder did. Choosing the structure uses manual reference control, which is experimental in Chrome 158: to use it, turn on chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features and relaunch Chrome.

    Manual reference control is experimental in Chrome 158. Turn on chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features and relaunch Chrome to design your own structure. Until then, you can try the two structures that the L1T1 and L1T2 scalability modes produce.
